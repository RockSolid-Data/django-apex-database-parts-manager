CSV Format

The CSV must have a header row. The part_number column is required and is used as the unique key (existing parts are updated, new ones are created).

Supported columns:

part_number, part_name, key, yt_number, j_and_n, oem_number, item_no, category, type, oem_type, item_typ, oem, primary_vendor, catalog, plug_id, price, cost_price, stock_quantity, reorder_qty, bin_number, description, foot_notes, superseding_notes, unit_number (links to Unit by unit_number).
